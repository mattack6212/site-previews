What the shop does, in its own words.

“We do mufflers, custom exhaust, tires, brakes, oil changes and mechanic work.”

That is how R & D describes itself on its own Facebook page, and it matches what customers describe in their reviews — spark plugs and belts, alternators and motor work, brakes, tires and oil.

  • Mufflers

    Gone quiet, gone loud, or rusted through. Muffler work is the first thing on the shop’s own list of what it does.

  • Custom exhaust

    A wall of pipe hangs in the bay. Exhaust is bent and fitted on site, which is not something every shop around here still does.

  • Tires

    Fitted and balanced. Tell them the size off the sidewall, or let them read it off the car.

  • Brakes

    Pads, rotors and the noise that brought you in. On the shop’s list alongside tires and oil.

  • Oil changes

    The routine job that keeps everything else honest. Booked on its own or done while the car is already in.

  • Mechanic work

    Reviewers describe spark plugs, belts, alternators, motor mounts and engine computers — the ordinary running repairs a daily driver needs.

Exhaust work still happens in the bay.

Pipe on the wall, a lift, and room for a project car. Plenty of shops stopped doing custom exhaust and started ordering parts in. R & D still lists mufflers and custom exhaust first.

If it has started droning, rattling at the joint, or sitting too loud at the lights, that is the kind of job this shop is set up for.
