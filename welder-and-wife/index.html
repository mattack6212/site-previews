What they do

From a handrail to a staircase

Everything below is work this shop is publicly documented doing — shown in their own photographs or described by their own customers. It is not a full menu. Call and ask.

Custom fabrication

One-off steel built to fit: curved diamond-plate staircases, frames, racks and structures made for a specific job rather than ordered from a catalogue.

Stairs & handrail

Diamond-plate treads, curved stringers and galvanised handrail systems — fabricated in the shop and welded out on site.

Ornamental & architectural

Porch and step railings, balustrades and exterior metalwork finished to be looked at as well as leaned on.

On-site & mobile repair

They come to the job. One customer describes him arriving ready to work and finishing in about an hour; another was met on a Sunday afternoon.

Aluminium welding

Aluminium is its own skill and not every shop takes it. A customer's aluminium boat repair is among their published reviews.

Equipment & machinery repair

Broken cast and steel components put back into service — including a mower differential picked up and welded up inside two hours.

Trailers & truck bodies

Mesh-sided bodies, cages, decks and trailer repair, fabricated and finished.

Custom & motorcycle work

Hardtails, seat frames, sissy bars and one-off brackets for builds that do not come with instructions.
