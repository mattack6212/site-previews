Why people drive to Trenton for it

Four things customers say again and again.

The prices

The banner on the building says Priced Right Everyday. Reviewers coming from out of town say they were shocked at the difference against what they pay at home.

Sizes nobody else stocks

One long-standing review calls out lumber in “sizes you don’t easily find at the big box stores” — which is usually the reason a project stalls.

Staff who know the stock

Customers repeatedly describe the counter staff as friendly, knowledgeable and willing to help find things — and say they’ll help load the big stuff.

Indoors and out

There is a store and there is a yard. Reviewers say to be ready to look through both — and that it’s worth the time.

Handwritten cards, not shelf tags.

That is what a surplus store looks like when it is working: a load comes in, it gets priced, it goes out on the floor. It also means the only reliable way to know whether something is still there is to pick up the phone.
