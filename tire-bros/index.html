Six things, done properly, in two bays on Lafayette Road.

Everything below is work this shop does — it's on the building, on the banner in the bay, or in the reviews people left afterwards.

  • New tires

    A fresh set, fitted and balanced. Tell us the size on the sidewall — or let us read it off the car — and what you want to spend.

  • Used tires

    Half the sign out front says USED TIRES for a reason. A good used set is often the difference between driving safely this week and putting it off.

  • Mounting & balancing

    Tires come off, tires go on, wheels get spun and weighted so the steering wheel sits still at highway speed.

  • Wheel alignment

    On the rack in the bay, with the heads clamped to all four wheels. Worth doing the day you put new rubber on it, so you don't scrub it off early.

  • Brakes

    There's a BRAKES sign hanging inside the bay and it isn't decoration. If it's grinding, squealing or pulling, bring it by.

  • Oil changes

    While it's in for tires, or on its own. It's on the banner in the bay alongside brakes and alignment.

Alignment rack in the bay, heads on the wheels.

The alignment happens here, not somewhere else.

Plenty of tire shops sell you a set and send you down the road for the alignment. There's a rack in the bay and the heads go on all four wheels, so the car that comes in for tires can leave tracking straight.

Pulling to one side, uneven wear across the tread, a steering wheel that sits crooked on a straight road — that's the conversation to have before you buy the tires, not after.
