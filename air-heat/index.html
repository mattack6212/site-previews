Common questions

Good to know

What areas do you serve?

We serve the entire Walker and Catoosa Counties as well as East Ridge, TN with high-quality heating and A/C services. Our shop is on N Highway 27 in Chickamauga.

Do you charge for an installation estimate?

No. Call (706) 375-5252 for a free installation estimate on our HVAC service.

Are your technicians certified?

Yes. All of our technicians are licensed and NATE-certified. NATE (North American Technician Excellence) requires passing a core HVACR exam plus a specialty exam, and technicians must re-qualify every five years.

What brands do you install and service?

American Standard, Lennox, Maytag, Mitsubishi and Tempstar.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — ask about our available warranties and financing options. We accept cash, check, Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express.

What is crawl space encapsulation?

Your crawl space affects your home’s comfort, air quality and long-term protection. Moisture, standing water and fungus underneath your home can lead to higher energy bills, musty smells and even structural issues over time. Air Heat now offers full crawl space services, including vapor barriers and dehumidifiers.
