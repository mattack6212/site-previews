What they do

Grooming, start to finish

Everything listed here is something a customer describes in a public Google review of this shop. Nothing is added.

Full groom & haircut

Cut to what you ask for — reviewers single out being listened to about the length they wanted, and getting a bandana or a bow on the way out.

Baths

In the stainless tub out back. One customer's dog "smelt good for almost 4 days" after; another's came home "so soft" and smelling of blueberry.

Nail trims

The single most-mentioned job in their reviews. Several people describe calling and being told to come on in the same day — and being allowed to stand with the dog while it is done.

Ears, teeth & glands

Ear cleaning, teeth brushing and anal-gland expression, booked on their own or added onto a groom.

De-matting & full shaves

Coats that have gone too long. One owner brought in an eight-year-old cocker spaniel "in really rough shape" after surgery kept her from brushing him.

Breeds of every size

Great Pyrenees, standard poodles, golden retrievers, corgis, schnauzers, shih tzus, cockers, maltipoos, yorkies and bulldogs all turn up by name in their reviews.

Why people drive past other shops

The dog that won't let anyone else do it

It is the thing their customers write about most, and they write about it at length: dogs that bite groomers, dogs that have never left the farm, rescues that flinch, fourteen-year-olds with bad knees. Over and over the reviews describe the same approach — slow down, let the owner stay, and don't rush the dog.

  • You can stay in the room the first time — more than one owner says they were invited to.
  • Photos through the appointment if you're anxious about leaving them.
  • A call the moment the dog is done, so it isn't waiting in a kennel for you.
  • Senior dogs taken slowly. One regular's dog has been coming here since he was two; he is fourteen now.
