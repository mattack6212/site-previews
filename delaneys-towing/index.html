Calling from the side of the road?

Have these ready and we can get a truck pointed your way faster. If you don't know some of it, that's fine — call anyway.

  • Where you are — a road name, a mile marker, a cross street, or just drop a pin.
  • What you're driving — make, model, and roughly how big.
  • What it's doing — won't start, won't steer, flat, locked up, or in a ditch.
  • Where it's going — your shop, your house, or you're not sure yet.
  • Are the wheels turning? — it changes how we load it.

What we haul

Flatbed first, because it's easier on the vehicle.

Both trucks are rollbacks. The car rides on the deck instead of hanging off the back, which matters a great deal if it's low, all-wheel-drive, damaged, or worth a lot of money.

Breakdowns

Won't start, won't move, overheated, transmission gone. We load it and take it where you want it — your shop, your mechanic, or your driveway.

Accidents & recovery

Collision work, rollovers, and vehicles that have left the road. If it's down a bank or through a guardrail, that's a winch job and we do those.

Classics & collector cars

Low, old, restored, or not currently running. Soft straps and a flat deck. Our own photos show the kind of thing we get asked to move.

Long-distance hauls

Not every tow is around the corner. We hold interstate operating authority, so a haul across the state line or further out is a normal job, not a special one.

Winch-outs

Off the shoulder, in the mud, over an embankment, or nose-down in a ditch. Sometimes the car is fine and it just needs pulling back onto the pavement.

Not sure what you need?

Call and describe it. If it's not a job for us we'll tell you that too, and point you at whoever can actually help.
