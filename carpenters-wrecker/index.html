Stranded right now

Four things to do while you wait

None of this costs anything and all of it makes the pickup faster and safer. Call first, then work down the list.

  1. 1

    Get off the travel lane if the car still rolls

    Shoulder, verge, exit ramp, car park — anywhere out of moving traffic. If it will not move, leave it and stay back from the roadway.

  2. 2

    Hazards on, and stay belted inside

    On a highway shoulder the car is the safest place to sit. Keep everyone in, doors shut, seatbelts fastened, and get out on the passenger side if you must get out at all.

  3. 3

    Find a landmark before you call

    A mile marker, an exit number, a cross street or the nearest business sign. A pin dropped in your phone's map works just as well and saves a lap of searching.

  4. 4

    Know where the car is going

    Your garage, your driveway, a body shop, home. Having the destination ready when the truck arrives means one trip instead of two.
