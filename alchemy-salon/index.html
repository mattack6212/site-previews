What we do

Color, cuts, pixies — and a tanning bed

A small salon that does a lot of color. Blondes, reds, lived-in brunettes, and short cuts done properly.

Color

The thing we do most. Full color, retouches, and the kind of dimensional work that grows out without a hard line.

Blonding

Lightening, toning, and keeping it that way — the service our regulars tell people about by name.

Cuts & pixies

Everyday cuts and short shapes. Pixies are on the list for a reason: they’re a cut not every salon wants to take on.

Occasion styling

Braided half-ups, curls and pinned looks for weddings, prom and anything else worth sitting still for.

Tanning

Yes, we tan too. Ask at the desk or on the phone.
