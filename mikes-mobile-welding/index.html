What we weld

If it's metal and it's broken, start with a phone call.

Most of what comes in is a repair that somebody has been told to replace instead. The list below is the work our customers describe in their own public reviews — it isn't everything we do, so if yours isn't on it, ask.

Trailers

Equipment trailers, boat trailers, cargo trailers — frames, spring hangers, tongues and the cracks that show up after a few thousand hard miles.

Mowers & equipment

Mower decks, broken frames, brackets and tractor parts. Often welded stronger than the factory seam that let go.

Railings, steps & gates

Handrails for porches and stairs, modern horizontal rail, cable rail, traditional ironwork, fences and gates — measured, built and installed.

Aluminium & stainless

Not every welder takes aluminium work. Spiral stairs, boat transoms, brackets and castings that most shops will turn away.

Vehicles & machines

Body and chassis frames, motorcycle parts, heavy equipment repairs and the odd job nobody else wanted to look at.

Custom fabrication

Racks, frames, brackets, stands — if you can describe it or sketch it, it can usually be built.

Two ways to do it

We come out, or you bring it in.

Whichever is less trouble for you. If the thing is broken enough that it can't be moved, that is exactly what the truck is for.

We come to you

The truck carries the welder, the bottles, the leads and the cutting gear, so the job gets done where it sits — your yard, your farm, your shop, the side of the road.

Or bring it to the shop

Smaller pieces are often quicker and cheaper handled at the shop. Call first, then run it by — a lot of small repairs get turned around the same day.
