What we do

A full-service hair salon — not just a trim.

In their own words, A&J “offers a wide variety of hair services.” In practice that means the colour work most small shops send you elsewhere for gets done here, in the chair, by the person who cut it.

Cuts, trims & restyles

Everyday cuts, clean-ups between appointments, and bigger changes when you are ready for one.

Colour

All-over colour, grey coverage, root touch-ups, and bold shades when that is what you are after.

Highlights & balayage

Dimension and lift, from a few brightening pieces through to a full hand-painted blend.

Styling & blowouts

Curls, waves and a proper finish — for an event, or just to walk out feeling put together.
