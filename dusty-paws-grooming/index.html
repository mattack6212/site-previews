What she does

Grooming, start to finish

Everything below is listed by the shop itself. If your dog only needs one thing — just the nails, just the ears — that’s a normal thing to ask for.

Baths

Washed, dried and brushed out. The part most dogs need most often, and it can be booked on its own.

All-over haircuts

A full clip to the length you ask for. One reviewer singled out the face trim; another, the work around the mouth and ears.

Nails, ears & glands

Nails trimmed, ears cleaned, glands expressed. Included in a groom, or booked by themselves as a quick visit.

Teeth

Teeth brushing is on her own list of services. Worth adding if nobody’s been near them in a while.

The spa add-ons

These are the extras she lists by name. Prices depend on the size of your dog — she’ll tell you when you ring.

  • Blueberry facial
  • Conditioner wrap
  • Foot balm

The blueberry facial is described by the shop as “a calming for anxious pets.”

About the shop

A one-groomer salon on Lafayette Road

Dusty Paws is a small shop in Fort Oglethorpe, just off Lafayette Road and a few minutes from the Chattanooga line. The grooming is done by the owner — which is why the reviews keep saying “she” and why your dog sees the same person every time.

In her own words, on her Yelp listing:

“I have 12 years of experience in the industry and enjoy creating lasting relationships with my customers. We offer Spa Packages, ranging in price, depending on the size of your dog. Call today!”
