Who we are
“We are just a group of great friends, doing some of the best hair you will ever see.” Bella Mi Salon, in its own words

The salon opened in 2008 and moved to its current space on Highway 27 in 2020 — a bigger floor, better light, and somewhere comfortable to wait.

The goal, as they put it, is “to make you feel comfortable and at home during your visit.” Eighteen years in a town this size means a lot of the chairs are filled by people who have been coming back for a decade or more.
