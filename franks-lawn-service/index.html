Grass cut, edges trimmed, grounds kept up

Most of our work is the ordinary, repeating kind: the same properties on a regular round, kept tidy so nobody has to think about it. Some of it is a one-off — a yard that has got away from somebody and needs rescuing in a hurry.

Regular mowing

Put your property on the round and it gets cut on a schedule. Several of the customers who have written about us online have been on it for four, five, six and nine years running.

Property maintenance

Keeping grounds presentable between cuts — trimming, edges, and the tidy-up afterwards so the place looks finished rather than just mown.

Commercial grounds

Business premises and multiple properties under one arrangement. Some of our customers have us looking after several addresses at once.

Overgrown yards

If it has got out of hand — a rental between tenants, a property that has sat, a letter from the city — call and tell us how bad it is. We have seen worse.

Yard waste clean-up

Clearing what comes off the property, rather than leaving it stacked at the kerb for somebody else to deal with.

Shrub trimming

Shrubs and hedges cut back and shaped, and taken out altogether when they have outgrown the spot they were planted in.
