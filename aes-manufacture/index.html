What comes through the bay door

Four kinds of work, one shop

Most places pick a lane. AES has always run both ends — the trailer that has to be back on the road tomorrow, and the piece that's never been built before.

Repairs

Trailers, tractors, mowers, pipe, boats and pontoons, hoppers, cast iron, automotive. Structural repairs on heavy equipment right down to a broken steel door.

Custom fabrication

Parts and assemblies built to your drawing or your description. Aluminum staircases, frames, brackets, flag poles, architectural bands and hardware.

Sculpture & art metal

Fabrication for working artists — we build other people's designs, to their intent, and we've done it for Chattanooga's metal and ceramic artists for years.

On-site work

Some jobs can't come to the shop. Installation and repair work out at the site where the steel already is.

Processes & metals

If it's metal, ask.

Three welding processes under one roof, across the three metals most jobs actually need. If you're not sure which your part needs, that's a thirty-second phone call, not a quote.
