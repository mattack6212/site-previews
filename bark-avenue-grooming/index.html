What we do

A full-service salon for cats and dogs of all breeds and sizes. Call for a price on your particular pet — coat, size and condition all change it, so we would rather quote you honestly than guess on a website.

Bathing

A proper wash and dry, for anything from a small house cat to a big hairy dog.

Haircuts & trimming

Breed trims or whatever style suits you and your pet. Poodles and doodles are a specialty.

Nail trimming

Quick, careful nail trims without the wrestling match.

Ear cleaning

Part of the groom, and one of the things most often skipped at home.

Your pet hangs out with us

This is the bit that makes us different from a chain grooming counter, and it is how we have always described it ourselves:

In our own words

“We are a pet grooming establishment, with 20 years experience in grooming. We specialize in bathing, trimming and pampering your pet in a friendly environment in which your pet hangs out with us in a daycare atmosphere.”

What that means on the day

No stack of crates and no waiting their turn in a box. Dogs are out with us and with each other between stages, which is why a lot of them stop dreading the trip.

Nervous or difficult pets

Tell us on the phone. A dog that is a handful elsewhere is often fine here, and we would rather know before you arrive than after.
