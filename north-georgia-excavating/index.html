What we do

From raw ground to a finished pad

Residential site prep is the core of the business — but if it involves moving dirt, cutting grade or hauling material, it is work we take on.

Site prep & building pads

Clearing, cutting and compacting house pads and home sites so the builder can start on solid, level ground.

Grading & leveling

Finish grading, yard leveling and shaping ground so water runs where it should instead of where it wants to.

Land clearing & tree removal

Taking raw or overgrown ground down to workable dirt, including dead and problem trees and stump removal.

Driveways

New driveways cut, shaped, based and rocked — plus repairs to washed-out and rutted drives.

Drainage & detention ponds

Diverting water away from homes and foundations: swales, ditch rock, pipe, and detention ponds.

Water lines & trenching

Trenching for water lines and utilities, backfilled and restored when the work is finished.

Septic

Septic work as part of a residential site package.

Erosion control

Erosion and water-conservation measures to keep a finished site from washing away in the first hard rain.

Rock, gravel, fill dirt & topsoil

Material supplied and hauled in — crushed stone, gravel, fill dirt and topsoil, delivered and placed.

Demolition & debris removal

Structures taken down and hauled off, including fire-damaged buildings, and sites cleaned back to grade.

Storm shelter installation

Excavation and installation of residential storm shelters.
