Why the finish is the whole job

Anyone can pour it. Finishing is what you live with.

It has to drain

Water that sits is water that freezes. A neighbour here specifically noted no puddles four years on.

Joints go where they go

Concrete cracks. Control joints decide whether it cracks where you planned or across the middle.

You only get one go

Concrete is the least forgiving thing on a property. Taking it back out is the expensive part.
