For your house

Garage doors, done properly the first time

Most calls are one of a handful of things. Springs break, openers die, rollers and cables wear out, and doors come off their track. Any of them will stop a car getting out of a garage.

Broken springs

The most common failure and the one that makes a door feel impossibly heavy overnight. It is also the one you should not try yourself.

Openers

Repair or replacement when the opener stops responding, runs but doesn't lift, or reverses halfway.

Manual to automatic

Have a door you still lift by hand? It can be converted and fitted with an opener.

Off-track and general repair

Rollers, cables, hinges, panels and alignment — the running repairs that keep a door working for years longer.

New doors, basic to fancy

New installation across the range, whether you want the plain one that works or something that changes the front of the house.

Not sure what's wrong?

Most people can't tell a spring from a cable from an opener fault, and there is no reason you should. Describe what it's doing on the phone.
