What we do

Heating and air, start to finish

Residential heating and cooling for homeowners across northwest Georgia and the Chattanooga area — from a single no-heat call to a full system changeout.

Heating repair & service

Furnaces and heat pumps that won't start, won't keep up, or keep shutting down. We diagnose the actual fault before we quote anything.

Air conditioning repair

Systems blowing warm, freezing up, or short-cycling. Cooling problems get looked at properly rather than guessed at.

Seasonal service

A check-over before the weather turns, so a small fault gets caught while it is still a small fault.

System replacement

When a unit really is at the end, we size and install the replacement — including the duct changes that go with it.

Ductwork & sheet metal

Plenums, transitions and takeoffs fabricated in our own shop, so the metal fits your house instead of the other way round.

Repair before replace

If a repair will genuinely get you there, we would rather do the repair and tell you how much life is left in the unit.

Our shop

We build our own metal

Plenty of heating-and-air outfits order their duct fittings in and make them fit. We cut, brake and assemble ours on McFarland Avenue — which is why an awkward attic or a tight crawlspace is a measuring job rather than a problem.
