How it works

First time? It’s four steps.

Nobody is born knowing how a scrap yard works. Here’s the whole thing, start to finish, so you know what to expect before you turn in.

Drive in

Come in off W Gordon Ave and follow the drive. Someone will point you where to go.

Get weighed

You cross the scale loaded. We weigh the vehicle, you unload, and we weigh you again empty.

We sort and grade

Metals are separated by type and grade. Our staff will help you get it off the truck.

Get paid

The difference in weight, at the day’s price for each grade, is what you’re owed.

Before you come

Worth knowing before you load up.

Two of these will put more money in your pocket. The third saves you a wasted trip.

Sort it before you get here

Keep your copper separate from your brass, your aluminium separate from your steel. Mixed loads take longer to work through, and metal that arrives already sorted gets graded as what it actually is.

“If you’re looking for a faster experience pre sort all your metals before arriving.” — Matt Wirick, Google review

Clean pays more than contaminated

Grading is about what’s actually in the load. Stainless with steel bolted to it, or copper still wrapped around something that isn’t copper, can’t be graded as clean material — it goes in at the lower grade, and that’s a real difference in what you take home.

If you’re not sure whether something counts as clean, ask before you load it. A two-minute phone call can be worth a lot on the scale.
