Two kinds of work, two licence categories

Georgia splits structural pest control into separate categories, and a company has to be licensed in each one it works in. We hold both of the ones that matter for a house.

Household pest control

The everyday things that get into a kitchen, a crawlspace or a storage building — ants, roaches, spiders, fleas, wasps and the rest of the list above. Homes and commercial premises alike.

Wood-destroying organisms

Termites and the other pests that go after the structure itself rather than the pantry. This is its own state licence category, separate from general pest work, and we are licensed for it.

Commercial & residential

Whether it is a family home, a rental, a shop or a storage building, the job gets looked at the same way. Call and tell us what you are seeing.

Licensed in Georgia, Alabama and Tennessee

Trenton sits in the corner of Georgia where the Alabama and Tennessee lines are both a short drive away. We are licensed on all three sides of it, so the state line does not decide whether we can help you.
