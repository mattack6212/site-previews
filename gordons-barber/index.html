What he does

Barbering, without the fuss

Haircut

Men's and boys' cuts, clippers or scissors. Customers single out his fades — one review names a high skin fade specifically.

Haircut & shave

The full visit: cut and shave together, in one chair, in one sitting.

Shave only

Come in for a shave on its own whenever you'd rather not do it yourself.

Pricing is straightforward — just call and ask. Reviewers have described it as “unbeatable” and “very fair,” and kids' cuts are priced below adults'.

The shop

One barber, one chair, no appointment required

Gordon's is a small shop on Ringgold Road, in the row of storefronts next to the carniceria. One barber, working the chair himself, six days a week.

There is no booking app and no waiting list. You walk in, you sit down, and you are back out again — one customer timed it at twenty-five minutes, start to finish. Another turned up with no appointment at all and still got squeezed in.

Children are welcome, there is a gumball machine by the counter, and card and tap-to-pay are both fine.
