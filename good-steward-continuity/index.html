If you could not work for 90 days, would the business know what to do?

Many strong businesses rely on one owner for critical relationships, decisions, passwords, banking, payroll, licensing, and day-to-day leadership. Without a documented plan, an unexpected event can place the company, employees, and family at risk. Planning now preserves choices later.

A prepared business can answer all seven

  • Someone has legal and operational authority
  • Payroll and banking can continue
  • Critical passwords and records are secure
  • Key employees know their roles
  • Customers and vendors receive the right communication
  • Family members understand their options
  • The business can be valued and transferred

The answer is not always “sell.”

Every owner, family, and company is different. We help evaluate the realistic paths forward:

  • Continue under existing leadership
  • Transition to a family member
  • Sell to key employees or management
  • Explore employee ownership
  • Prepare for an outside sale
  • Merge with another operator
  • Preserve valuable assets and close responsibly

Our process

A clear process when the situation feels complicated

  1. Understand

    Clarify the owner’s goals, family situation, business condition, risks, authority, and urgency.

  2. Protect

    Secure critical operations, information, people, relationships, and immediate business value.

  3. Evaluate

    Assess continuity, transferability, financial condition, leadership options, and potential market value.

  4. Coordinate

    Bring in the appropriate attorney, CPA, valuation professional, broker, lender, operator, or insurance advisor.

  5. Transition

    Execute the selected plan with clear responsibilities, documentation, communication, and accountability.
