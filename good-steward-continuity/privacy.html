Information we collect

Good Steward Continuity collects only the information you choose to provide through a contact or resource form: your name, contact details, company, location, role, and a general description of your situation. We also may collect basic, privacy-friendly analytics about site usage.

Note for this preview: the forms on this site are demonstrations. They are not connected to any server, list, or database, and they transmit and store nothing.

What we ask you not to send

Please do not submit detailed health information, Social Security numbers, bank records, tax returns, passwords, or confidential estate documents through this website. A secure process for exchanging documents is established at the start of an engagement.

How we use information

To respond to your inquiry, to understand whether we can help, to coordinate with professionals you authorize us to speak with, and—if you opt in—to send occasional continuity and succession resources by email.

How we share information

We do not sell your information. We share it only with your authorization, with service providers who operate our systems under confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.

Email consent and unsubscribe

Marketing email is sent only to people who opt in. Every message will include an unsubscribe link, and we will honor unsubscribe requests promptly.

Cookies and analytics

This preview site sets no cookies and makes no external requests. A production site would use privacy-friendly analytics, and cookie disclosure will be added where required.

Data retention and deletion

A written retention and deletion process will be published here once it is established, along with instructions for requesting deletion of your information.

Contact

Contact routing for privacy requests is not yet published. Until it is, use the contact page.
