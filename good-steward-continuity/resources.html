The 90-Day Owner Absence Checklist

The questions every owner should answer before retirement, illness, disability, or an unexpected emergency.

A working list you can take through your own company in an afternoon. It covers the areas that create the most disruption when an owner is suddenly unavailable:

  • Authority
  • Banking and payroll
  • Passwords and systems
  • Key employees
  • Customers and vendors
  • Licenses and insurance
  • Contracts and leases
  • Family communication
  • Professional advisors
  • Succession and sale options

The checklist educates. It does not provide legal documents or personalized legal advice.
