The shop

Built on purpose, booked by appointment

Barbería Brawn sits in the row of units at 6802 Ringgold Road, in East Ridge. Inside it is black cabinetry, brass-based chairs and a hexagon-lit ceiling — a room that has clearly been fitted out on purpose rather than inherited.

It runs on appointments rather than walk-ins, so the chair is yours when you arrive and there is no sitting around waiting for one. Reviewers mention the same two things over and over: the barbers take their time, and the lines come out clean.

Children are welcome, and there is a restroom on site.

Beyond the fade

Braids and colour, booked ahead

Alongside cuts and beard work, the shop books longer appointments for braids and for colour. These are not squeeze-in jobs — braids run two and a half hours, and a dye-and-cut runs three.

If that is what you are after, book it rather than turning up, so the time is set aside for you.
