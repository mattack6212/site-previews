We do more than just alignments.

That line is printed on our own business card, and it's there because the name on the sign only tells you half the story. Everything below is work this shop does.

  • Four-wheel alignment

    The thing we're named for. If the car pulls, the wheel sits crooked on a straight road, or a new set of tires is wearing unevenly, start here.

  • Wheel balancing

    A shake that only shows up at highway speed is usually balance, not alignment. We'll put it on the machine and show you what it's doing.

  • Brakes

    Pads, rotors and lines, inspected properly with the wheels off. If they're fine, we'll tell you they're fine.

  • Front end & steering

    Tie rods, pitman arms, the loose and worn parts that make an alignment fall straight back out of spec. The alignment is only as good as what's holding it.

  • Tune-ups

    Routine maintenance to keep it running right, on the card alongside the brakes and the A/C since long before any of us were driving.

  • Air conditioning

    Blowing warm in a Georgia August is not something to live with. A/C is on the card too — call and tell us what it's doing.

A car that tracks straight is a car that stops eating tires.

Pulling to one side, a steering wheel that sits off-centre on a straight road, or tread wearing faster on one edge than the other — those aren't separate problems. They're the same problem, and left alone they turn a cheap adjustment into a new set of tires.

If someone has already rebalanced it and it still shakes at sixty, that's worth bringing here. That exact job is most of what we do.
