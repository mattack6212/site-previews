What Jeff Does

If it's on your list, call Jeff

Repairs, installs, touch-ups and the odd jobs that have been bugging you for months. Here's the short version of what he handles.

Drywall Repair

Holes, dents, cracks, water spots and doorknob punches, patched, textured and ready for paint.

Painting

Rooms, trim, doors, cabinets and exterior touch-ups. Walls, ceilings, trim and the rooms you've been meaning to refresh.

Carpentry & Trim

Baseboard, casing, crown touch-ups, shelving, small built-ins and the rotten board that needs replacing.

Doors & Windows

Sticking or sagging doors, new knobs and deadbolts, weatherstripping, screens, and windows that won't open or close right.

Deck & Fence Repair

Loose or rotten boards, wobbly railings, steps, gates that drag, and fence sections that took a hit.

TV Mounting & Shelves

TVs mounted level and into studs, floating shelves, mirrors, curtain rods and heavy art hung right the first time.

Furniture Assembly

Beds, dressers, desks, cribs, grills and outdoor sets. Put together, leveled, and ready to use.

Gutter Cleaning

Leaves and gunk cleared, downspouts flushed, and loose hangers or sagging runs re-secured.

Fixture Swaps

Replacing faucets, showerheads, toilet flappers and fill valves, cabinet hardware, and existing light fixtures, like for like.

Caulk & Weatherproofing

Tubs, showers, sinks, windows and siding gaps re-caulked. Drafts sealed before the cold weather hits.

Honey-Do Lists

Ten little things you've been meaning to get to? Hand Jeff the list and knock it all out in one visit.

Move-In & Rental Punch Lists

Getting a house ready to sell, rent or move into: patch, paint, fix, adjust, replace. One call covers the whole walkthrough list.

Questions

Good to know

How fast will I hear back?
Fast. That's the whole point. If Jeff can't pick up because he's in the middle of a job, he'll call you back. You won't be chasing him for days just to get a quote.
Is my job too small?
No. One loose cabinet door is a real job. And if you've got a few small things, group them into one visit and get them all done at once.
Do you give free estimates?
Yes. Call (603) 630-2315, describe the job, and Jeff will tell you what it'll take and what it'll cost before any work starts.
Do you do electrical and plumbing work?
Jeff handles simple swaps: replacing a faucet, a showerhead, toilet parts, or an existing light fixture like for like. Anything that needs a licensed electrician, plumber or HVAC tech, like new circuits, moving pipes or gas lines, he'll tell you so up front and point you in the right direction.
What areas do you cover?
North Georgia and the Chattanooga area: Ringgold, Fort Oglethorpe, Rossville, Chickamauga, LaFayette, Dalton, Chattanooga, East Ridge, Red Bank, Hixson, Ooltewah, Signal Mountain and the surrounding communities. If you're close, just call and ask.
Can you take care of a whole list in one trip?
That's the best way to use a handyman. Write down everything that's been bugging you, walk Jeff through it, and he'll give you one price for the lot.
