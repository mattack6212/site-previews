Before you set off

How to find the yard

Don't trust your GPS on the last few miles.

Drivers keep reporting that satnav routes them down the wrong roads into the valley. Use the route below instead.

  1. Come in on Highway 2.
  2. Turn onto Happy Valley Road — stay on it.
  3. Happy Valley Road takes you onto Dry Valley Road. The yard is on Dry Valley.
  4. Do not try to cut through on Ridgeland or Dogwood.
Heads up for tractor-trailers.

The approach is narrow with tight turns. Drivers advise bringing your tandems forward for maximum manoeuvrability before you make the turn in.

Checking in

  1. Park out of the way.
  2. Go up the stairs next to the first dock.
  3. Keep left — you'll see the offices.
  4. Check in there. They'll bring the paperwork out to you when you're finished.

What we do

Textile recycling

Textile recycling
