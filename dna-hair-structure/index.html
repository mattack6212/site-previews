What customers actually wrote

Reproduced as written from the salon’s public Google reviews, and attributed to the people who wrote them. Nothing here was solicited, edited for flattery, or made up.

The thing people keep saying

Read this salon’s reviews end to end and the same two patterns come up again and again: people stay for years, and people arrive after somebody else got it wrong.

One customer writes that she had been going since high school and still books here “even though I don’t even live in town anymore.” Another says she had been away six years, came home, made an appointment, and “I’m looking good again.”

Several describe arriving in a mess made somewhere else — brassy roots, dead ends, hair that had been breaking off for three years — and leaving fixed. One woman who had not let anyone touch her hair in two decades left a five-star review.

And more than one mentions the room itself: “a very comfortable atmosphere… They make you feel like family.”

Those are customers’ words, not the salon’s. No price is quoted anywhere on this page, because the salon publishes none and only the salon can tell you what your appointment will cost. Ask when you ring.
