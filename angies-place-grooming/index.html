How it works

One dog at a time. That's the whole idea.

Most grooming shops run several dogs through at once, which means yours waits in a crate before its turn and again afterwards. Here, yours is booked on its own.

That is why the week is short and the appointments are spaced out — and it is why customers keep mentioning how quickly they get their dog back.

It is also why she takes on the dogs other places struggle with. In her own words: “I specialize in taking care of those that don't do well in a regular grooming setting. Meaning they need more one on one attention, or they don't do well around other peoples animals.”
