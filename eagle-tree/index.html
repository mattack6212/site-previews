What we do

Six services, one crew

The list below is the company's own, in its own order and its own words.

Tree Removal

Whole trees taken down in sections when there is no room to drop them — between a shed and a power line, or leaning over a roof.

Tree Trimming

Cutting back limbs that have grown over a roof, a driveway, a fence line or a neighbour's property.

Tree Pruning

Shaping and thinning to keep a tree healthy and to take weight off the limbs most likely to fail.

Deadwooding

Clearing out dead and rotting limbs before they come down on their own.

Storm Damage Cleanup

Clearing what a storm has already brought down, and taking out what it left unsafe.

Lot / Land Clearing

Clearing a building lot or opening up overgrown acreage, with the brush hauled off.

Stump grinding is handled too — it is named in the company's Google listing and in a September 2026 customer review.

After a storm

A tree on the ground is only half the problem.

The one still standing with a split trunk or a cracked limb is the other half. Storm damage cleanup is one of the six services the company lists, and it covers both — hauling off what came down and taking out what the weather left unsafe.
