What we handle

Residential and commercial pest control

Every plan is backed by the Pest-Free Guarantee: if you see a pest between scheduled visits, we come back and re-treat at no extra charge.

General pest control

Ants, spiders, wasps, roaches and the other common household pests, treated on a bi-monthly exterior schedule.

Termite & wood-destroying insects

Termite and WDI protection, including carpenter ant coverage, available on the Gold Blend plan and above.

Mosquito & no-see-um control

Monthly treatments through mosquito season so the yard is usable again, plus exterior flea and tick work.

Rodent control

Mice and rats: exclusion, trapping and monitoring. Available as an add-on to any plan.

Commercial

Scheduled pest management for commercial properties across the service area. Call to talk through what your site needs.

Free inspection

No-obligation inspection before anything is quoted or treated, at either office or at your property.
