What customers actually wrote

Reproduced as written from the shop’s public Google reviews, and attributed to the people who wrote them. Nothing here was solicited, edited for flattery, or made up.

The thing people keep saying

Google sorts this shop’s reviews into topics, and the biggest one it finds is “honest mechanic”. The next are “reliable” and “honest staff”. Read the reviews end to end and the pattern is the same: people describe getting a phone call with a number in it before any extra work was done.

One customer writes that they were quoted $1,700 plus tax elsewhere for an alternator and water pump, and that the work was done here for $840 — “he did the work for what he said.”

Another says the bill came in “MUCH MUCH LOWER than we expected” and that the car was ready the next day. A third, a realtor who found the shop by accident while showing a house nearby, was told the noise in their truck was “a very simple fix” — and said so in a five-star review.

Those are customers’ words, not the shop’s. No price is quoted on this page, because every job is different and only the shop can tell you what yours will cost. Ask for the number up front — by every account on the listing, you will get a straight one.
