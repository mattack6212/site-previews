Exhaust work. That's the trade.

Everything below is work customers have described having done here, in their own reviews. If what's wrong with yours isn't on the list, call and ask — it's a short conversation.

Mufflers

Replaced or repaired. Stock or aftermarket — one customer came in to have a loud aftermarket muffler swapped back to a quiet factory one, and went home the same visit.

Exhaust pipes

Rusted-out pipe sections cut out and replaced. One customer had the muffler and the pipe both gone and had both done in a single visit.

Flex pipes

The braided section that cracks and blows. A hole in a flex line is a common, cheap, fast repair — and it's the one a reviewer described being in and out of here for.

Catalytic converters

Including after a theft. One customer had hers cut off twice and came here both times — she described him as kind and understanding about it, which is not nothing when it happens to you.

Custom & dual exhaust

Duals and polished tips, fitted on the same lift as the repairs. Whether you want it loud or want it quiet, say so when you pull in.

Welded on site

It's cut and welded in his own bay, not bolted together out of a box. That's the difference between a patch that holds and one that rattles loose by winter.

Most of it happens while you stand there.

Exhaust work is one of the few jobs left on a modern car that a good hand can diagnose on the lift and finish the same visit. You don't book it in for a week. You don't need a courtesy car.

Three different customers, on three different sites, over five years, have independently written down how long they were here. One said twenty minutes. One said less than an hour. One said “less than 1hr.” Nobody is promising you a time — your car is your car — but that's the shape of the work.
