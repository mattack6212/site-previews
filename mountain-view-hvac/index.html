Frequently asked questions

How often should I have my HVAC system serviced?

At least twice a year — once before summer for your air conditioning, and once before winter for your heating system.

What are the signs that my system needs repair?

Unusual noises, inconsistent temperatures, higher energy bills, and poor airflow.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Yes — all our technicians are fully certified, licensed and insured.

How long does an HVAC system typically last?

With proper maintenance, most systems last between 10 and 15 years, depending on usage and brand quality.

Do you offer emergency repair?

Yes — we provide emergency repair service to get your comfort restored as quickly as possible. Call (423) 556-8671.

What types of systems do you install?

Central air conditioning, ductless mini-splits, heat pumps and furnaces, matched to your home.

How can I reduce my energy costs?

Regular maintenance, cleaning or replacing filters, sealing ductwork, proper insulation and a programmable thermostat all help.
