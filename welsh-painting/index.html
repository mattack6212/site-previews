What we do

Residential, commercial, industrial.

We price every job off the same walkthrough: what is on the surface now, what has to come off, and what goes back on.

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, trim and doors — with dust containment and floor protection in place before anything gets opened.

Exterior painting

Siding, soffits, fascia and porches, scheduled around the weather so the coatings actually cure properly.

Cabinet painting

Starts with degreasing and deglossing — because cabinet finishes fail at the prep stage far more often than at the paint stage.

Deck staining

Cleaning, brightening and sealing before the stain ever goes down, so the colour holds instead of lifting in a season.

Commercial painting

Offices, retail space, multi-family properties and churches — often worked after hours so you don’t lose a trading day.

Industrial coatings

Protective coatings for warehouses, plants and structural steel, specified for the environment they have to survive.

Before you call

Questions we get asked most.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We carry full contractor liability insurance through State Farm and are licensed with the city, state and county.

What paint do you use?

Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams premium paints, plus premium-grade brushes, rollers, caulk and other products on every project. Ask us which ones we’ll use on yours — we’re happy to talk it through.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes — a guarantee in accordance with the manufacturer’s warranty, which is normally 2–3 years. In practice you should expect a great paint job to last somewhere between 12 and 15 years, as long as routine home maintenance is kept up.

How much does it cost to paint a house?

Most homeowners pay between $2,500 and $8,000, depending on size, condition and scope. Interior projects typically run $2,000–$6,000 and exteriors $3,000–$8,000. Every home is different, so we provide a free, itemized estimate before any work begins.

Can I pay by card?

Yes. We securely process card transactions through Payroc, with a 3% service fee.

How long have you been doing this?

Since 1987. We’ve completed hundreds of paint and home enhancement jobs for homeowners and businesses around Chattanooga, and you can read what those customers say on Google — 5.0 stars across 50 reviews.
