Paint and body. That's the whole trade.

Everything below is work this shop does. If what's wrong with your car isn't on the list, call and ask — it's a short conversation and it costs nothing.

  • Collision repair

    From a parking-lot crease to a front end that doesn't look like a front end any more. Panels straightened or replaced, gaps set back where they belong.

  • Painting & refinishing

    Colour matched to the panels either side of it, laid down and cleared so the repair stops being something you can point at.

  • Bumpers & rear-end damage

    The most common job in the building. Covers repaired or replaced, brackets and absorbers put back, everything lined up again.

  • Lights & lenses

    Headlights, tail lights and the housings behind them — the parts that get broken first and that will fail an inspection on their own.

  • Steering & suspension

    A hit hard enough to bend a panel is often hard enough to move something underneath it. Worth looking at before you put a new bumper on.

  • Insurance claims

    We'll talk to your adjuster, write the supplement when the estimate misses something, and push back when the parts list isn't right.

The repair is our job. So is the argument about the repair.

Nobody plans a collision, and the worst part usually isn't the damage — it's the week afterwards, on hold, explaining the same thing to a different person each time, trying to work out whether the number you've been quoted is the right one.

That's the part we take off you. We'll deal directly with the adjuster, and if the estimate leaves something out, we'll say so and put it in writing. One of our customers put it better than we can: we were able to talk her insurer out of writing the car off.

How it goes.

No forms to fill in before anyone will speak to you, and no app to download.

  1. 1

    Call and tell us what happened

    What was hit, roughly how hard, and whether it's driveable. That's enough to work out what happens next.

  2. 2

    Bring it by and we'll look at it

    We'll go over the damage with you in the lot, including the parts of it you can't see from the outside.

  3. 3

    We take it from there

    If there's a claim, we'll deal with the adjuster directly. We'll tell you where it stands rather than leaving you to chase it.
