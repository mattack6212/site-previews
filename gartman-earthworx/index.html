What We Do

Dirt work, start to finish

One crew, one number, one company accountable for the whole scope — clearing through final grade.

Site Work

Full site preparation for residential builds and commercial developments — pad prep, cut and fill, and the dirt package that everything else sits on.

Mass Grading

Large-scale earthmoving and precision finish grading. Machine control and experienced operators mean the grade is right before anyone pours.

Land Clearing

Brush, timber, stumps and debris removed and hauled off. Multi-acre tracts or a single building lot — cleared clean and ready to work.

Septic Installation

Complete septic systems installed under a Tennessee Division of Water Resources license — tank set, field laid, and trenched to spec.

Demolition

Structures, slabs, old foundations and hardscape taken down, loaded out and hauled away. Clean site, nothing left behind.

Sewer, Storm & Utilities

Sanitary sewer, storm lines and utility trenching — including large-diameter mains. Bedded, laid at grade, backfilled and compacted.

Drainage & Erosion Control

Water routed off the property instead of through it. French drains, swales, silt fence and stabilization that holds after the first hard rain.

Retaining Walls & Hardscape

Engineered block and stone retaining walls that hold a slope for good, plus the hardscape that finishes the space around them.

Concrete & Foundations

Footings, slabs, flatwork and foundation prep poured off our own grade — no handoff gap between the dirt work and the concrete.

Who You're Hiring

Owner-run. Licensed. On the job.

Gartman Earthworx LLC is owned and run by Silas Gartman out of Chattanooga, Tennessee. What started as one man and a machine now runs a crew and a fleet of heavy iron across Tennessee and North Georgia — and the standard hasn't moved: quality, integrity, on time, and within budget.

The work runs the full range, from residential lots to multi-acre commercial developments. Same crew, same standard, either way.

  • Tennessee Division of Water Resources Licensed with TDEC / Division of Water Resources — license 14818. The license that governs septic system installation in Tennessee.
  • Tennessee Contractor License TN Department of Commerce & Insurance — license 84583, current through December 2027.
