Licensing

You can check our certification yourself.

Georgia does not let just anyone put in a septic system or pump a tank. The Department of Public Health publishes the list of certified companies, county by county, and anybody can read it.

Georgia DPH · Walker County

Listed as Installer and Pumper

Georgia DPH records three certification types — Installer, Pumper, and Installer and Pumper. Most companies on the Walker County list hold one of the first two. We are listed under the third, which covers both putting a system in and pumping it out.

Pumping a septic tank in Georgia also requires a county septage removal permit, so this is not a slogan — it is a record you can look up.

Read it for yourself on the state’s own page — find Walker County, then our name:
dph.georgia.gov/environmental-health/onsite-sewage
